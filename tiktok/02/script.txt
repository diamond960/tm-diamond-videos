Your Windows 11 PC is slow, and it's not your fault.
Here are six free fixes, no extra apps needed.
Stay for fix six, most people skip it.
Fix one: press Control, Shift and Escape to open Task Manager.
Click Startup apps.
Select an app you don't need, and click Disable.
Now it won't launch every time you sign in.
Fix two: click Processes, then the CPU column.
The busiest app jumps to the top.
If you don't need it, select it and click End task.
Fix three: is your taskbar frozen?
Select Windows Explorer, and click Restart task.
The taskbar reloads without restarting your PC.
Fix four: open Settings, Accessibility, Visual effects.
Turn off Animation effects and Transparency effects.
Menus open without the extra motion.
Fix five: go to System, Storage, Storage Sense.
Turn it on, and pick how often it runs.
It clears temporary files for you, automatically.
Fix six: open Apps, then Installed apps.
Sort by size, large to small.
Click the three dots on an app you never use, and choose Uninstall.
Save this so your PC stays fast.
Follow for more Windows tips.
And if it feels slow again, start over with fix one.
