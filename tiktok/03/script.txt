Your Windows PC is slow, and here are six free fixes in one minute.
Number six is the one most people never find.
Fix one: press Control, Shift, Escape, and open Startup apps.
Disable the apps you don't need when your PC starts.
Fix two: on Processes, sort by CPU.
If one app is eating your processor, save your work and click End task.
Fix three: if the taskbar freezes, restart Windows Explorer instead of your PC.
Your open apps stay open.
Fix four: in Settings, open Storage Sense and turn on automatic cleanup.
Set it to run every week, and Windows deletes junk files for you.
Fix five: in Installed apps, sort by size.
Uninstall the big apps you never use, but only ones you recognize.
Fix six: search for performance, and open Adjust the appearance and performance of Windows.
Choose Adjust for best performance, then click OK.
Windows looks a little plainer, but it responds faster.
None of these fixes delete your files.
