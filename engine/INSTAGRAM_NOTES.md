# Instagram notes (@tmdiamond_)

What has worked on Instagram so far. The Instagram task adds a dated line after each 7 AM run; the Shorts
autopilot reads this before picking topics, hooks and lengths.

## Baseline - 2026-11-05 (81 followers, 842 views in the last 30 days, 12 Reels)

- Best by views: "5 Windows Tricks Most PC Users Don't Know" (70 s, 179 plays, 14.8% watched),
  "How to change your Windows password" (12 s, 165 plays, 34.6% watched), "5 ChatGPT Features You Probably
  Didn't Know" (70 s, 149 plays), "Windows privacy settings" (16 s, 145 plays), "Build your own app without
  code" (17 s, 127 plays, 27% watched).
- Worst: "Watch this before your next job interview" (63 s, 24 plays, 87.5% skip), "How to fix files that won't
  delete" (10 plays), "Windows shutdown shortcuts" (124 plays but 86.9% skip, 1.7 s average watch).
- Pattern: people watch about 3.5-13 seconds whatever the length, so 60-75 s Reels finish at only 7-15%
  while 12-17 s Reels reach 22-35%. Skip rates are 55-87%, so the first second decides everything.
  Windows tips and ChatGPT/AI tips get the most plays; off-niche topics (job interviews) flop.
- Do: 15-35 s, a hook in the first second, Windows + AI tool tips, keyword-rich captions (the old captions only
  used #reels #viral, which brings no search traffic).
- 2026-11-06 (7 AM NY): Best: "Your PC takes forever to start" 38 s, 139 views, 22.9% watched; "Stop losing the things you copy" 36 s, 149 views, 15.1%; "5 Windows Tricks Most PC Users Don't Know" 70 s, 179 views, 14.8%. Worst: "How to fix windows files won't delete" ?s, 10 views, n/a; "Watch this before your next job interview" 63 s, 24 views, 8.3%; "My YouTube channel posts videos by itself" 52 s, 25 views, 22.5%. Pattern: problem-first Windows fixes (startup, clipboard) under 40 s hold the most attention and views, while off-niche (interviews) and self-referential pipeline topics flop regardless of watch %; 60-75 s Reels drop to 7-15% watched.
