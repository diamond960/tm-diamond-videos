Your PC takes forever to start... and this is why.
Every app you install wants to launch with Windows.
And each one slows your startup down.
Here's the fix.
Press Control, Shift, Escape to open Task Manager.
Click Startup apps.
Look at the Startup impact column.
See an app marked High that you don't need right away?
Right-click it... and choose Disable.
Restart, and feel the difference.
