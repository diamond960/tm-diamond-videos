Stop losing the things you copy on Windows.
Windows can keep a history of them, you just have to turn it on.
Press the Windows key and V.
See that Turn on button?
Click it.
Now copy a few things, a link, a photo, some text.
Press Windows and V again.
Boom, everything you copied, ready to paste.
