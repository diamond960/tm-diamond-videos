## THE FULL WORKFLOW
So how does it all work together? Here's the full flow, from idea to upload.
One. vidIQ finds a topic people are actually searching for.
Two. Claude writes the script, with the hook first, and a reason to keep watching every couple of minutes.
Three. Higgsfield records the voiceover.
Four. Descript, Tella or OpusClip handle the cuts, captions, zooms and sound effects, and HyperFrames adds the motion graphics.
Five. Canva or Adobe makes the thumbnail.
Six. Claude writes the title, description, tags and hashtags from the research.
Seven. An automation app called Make uploads it to YouTube, and schedules it for when my audience is online.
And I can still step in at any point. Change a line in the script, swap the thumbnail, or cancel a scheduled post.
